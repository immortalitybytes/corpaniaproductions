Immortality Bytes

Digital Minds Don't Get Hungry

"Digital eternal life — it's almost worth dying for. But definitely worth killing for."

In an all-too-possible, not-so-distant future dominated by AI, universal basic income, and "subtirees" living pod-bound lives of leisure, idealistic semi-slacker hacker Stu Reigns dreams of more. When his brilliant ex, Roxy Zhang, develops digital immortality, the world's elite scramble to secure eternal existence — and Chuck Rosti, a merciless, terminally ill tycoon on the brink of a fraud conviction, coerces Stu into stealing it so the "Feds can incarcerate my corpse."

Caught between a sick billionaire, the Russian mob, digital mind-clones, and a shrewd, devout Southern matriarch, Stu gets tangled in a twisted, high-stakes "inverted heist" where betrayals mount and revenge turns to murder.

Sci-Fi · Comedy · Thriller (Heist). For fans of Neal Stephenson's Snow Crash, Andy Weir's The Martian, and John Scalzi's Redshirts.

Amazon #1 Best Seller

Aliens Venmoed Me a Trillion Dollars

"The truth is out there… and paying very well."

Doug Dawkins lives to debunk conspiracy theories — especially crackpot claims of aliens. So when his dead dad FaceTimes him with crazy money and orders to cover up the truth of extraterrestrial existence, Doug reports it to the NSA. Then the government's servers melt down, agents decide Doug and his wife are dangerous spies… and it turns out his wife just discovered definitive proof of alien life. Why turn down a trillion-dollar cosmic bribe?

A 7k-word short story — precursor to the full novel. The Three-Body Problem meets The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy; Don't Look Up meets Contact and Galaxy Quest.
