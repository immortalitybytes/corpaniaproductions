Award-winning, fun stories & bold visions for film, television, web series, and novels.

Daniel Lawrence Abrams is an award-winning screenwriter, director & producer of television (one Emmy-nominated) & web series (one an Official Honoree in the Webby Awards), and feature films (accepted by 10+ festivals and 2 theatrically released nationwide). His debut novel, IMMORTALITY BYTES, earned 12+ accolades, including five 1st-place wins in book competitions. His second book, ALIENS VENMOED ME A TRILLION DOLLARS, was a #1 Amazon Bestseller in four sub-genres and #2 in all of Sci-Fi.

Filmmaker

Daniel Lawrence Abrams: screenwriter, novelist, playwright, director & producer of documentaries, game shows, talk shows, competition & reality TV, and feature films.

Trained in comedy at The Groundlings, The Second City, and Whole World Theatre, Daniel has performed stand-up nationwide — from The Improv in Hollywood to The Comedy Cellar in New York — delivered a TEDx talk, and moderated panels for the Producers Guild of America. His live theater shows have been performed at The Stella Adler Theatre, The Powerhouse Theater, and the HBO/Warner Bros. Television Workspace.
